Ask my portfolio

A small version of how I build RAG systems. It runs in your browser with no LLM, so every answer comes word for word from passages it retrieved, and you can see each step.

How it works
  1. Route: keyword rules send the question to a projects, experience, skills, education or contact agent.
  2. Guard: questions with nothing relevant on this page are refused rather than guessed.
  3. Retrieve: BM25 and character-trigram similarity each rank 20 passages from this page.
  4. Fuse: reciprocal rank fusion merges the two rankings, boosted for the routed agent.
  5. Answer: the top passages, with citations limited to what was retrieved.
